Single player and Holomatch for Apple Silicon Macs.
No game data included: requires your own retail copy of the game.

Based on VoyagerSP-Android by imjustadudegamer,
lilium-voyager by Zack Middleton, ioquake3, id Software's Quake III Arena,
the Quake3e Vulkan renderer by Eugene C., and Raven Software's
Elite Force single-player game source.
